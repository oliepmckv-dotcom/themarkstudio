You know it doesn't reflect the quality of your work. You've been meaning to fix it for months. Every time you send the link, you add a disclaimer. Why it matters: Your website is often the first thing a potential client sees. If you're embarrassed by it, they can tell. 02 Your logo was made in Canva in 20 minutes. It's a standard font with your name in it. Maybe a generic icon. You know it looks amateur but told yourself you'd replace it later. Later never came. Why it matters: A logo built in 20 minutes looks like it was built in 20 minutes. It signals that you don't take your brand — or your business — seriously. 03 You use different fonts everywhere. Your Instagram uses one font, your emails use another, your proposals use whatever Word defaulted to. Nothing feels connected. Why it matters: Font inconsistency is one of the fastest ways to look unprofessional. A cohesive brand uses two or three fonts and sticks to them everywhere. 04 Your colour palette is "whatever looked good at the time." You picked a colour you liked when you started and added others as needed. Now your brand uses six different shades and nothing matches. Why it matters: Colour is the fastest visual signal your brand sends. An inconsistent palette makes your brand look unintentional — even if everything else is polished. 05 You have no brand style guide. If someone asked you to write down your exact brand colours, fonts and logo rules right now, you couldn't. There's nothing documented anywhere. Why it matters: A style guide isn't just for big companies. Any solopreneur working with a VA or posting consistently needs a single reference point. 06 Your brand doesn't match your price point. You charge premium prices for your services but your brand looks budget. There's a disconnect that potential clients feel even if they can't articulate it. Why it matters: People judge price by perception. A brand that looks £500 makes it hard to charge £5,000. Premium positioning starts with premium visuals. 07 Your Instagram grid looks inconsistent. Some posts are dark, some light. Different fonts, different layouts, different energy. Nothing ties it together as a single coherent brand. Why it matters: Your grid is a first impression. When someone lands on your profile and it looks inconsistent, they leave before they read a single caption. 08 You avoid showing your brand to people you respect. You don't link your website in certain networking situations. You skip adding Instagram to your email signature. You're hiding your brand because you know it's not good enough yet. Why it matters: Hiding your brand is losing business. Every time you hold back a link, you're leaving potential clients on the table. 09 Your brand doesn't feel like you. It was put together quickly, or copied from someone else's aesthetic, or just evolved by accident. It doesn't represent who you actually are or what you stand for. Why it matters: The best brands are an authentic expression of the person behind them. When your brand feels like you, selling becomes easier. 10 You've been meaning to "sort your brand" for over 6 months. It's been on the list. You've looked at designers. You've started Canva files that went nowhere. The intention is there but nothing has changed. Why it matters: A brand problem doesn't fix itself.
